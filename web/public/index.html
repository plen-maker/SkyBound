Features

Everything you need
in the air.

Built for serious simmers. No bloat, no subscriptions — just the tools you need.

📄
SimBrief OFP
Load your full flight plan instantly. Weights, fuel breakdown, route, navlog — simplified or full realistic SimBrief document format.
📡
Live Bridge
1Hz real-time telemetry from MSFS via SimConnect or FSUIPC7. GS, altitude, V/S, position — synced to your phone and desktop instantly.
🔔
Push Alerts
Set waypoint triggers that notify your phone. Top of Descent, fix crossing, landing — stay heads-up without watching the screen.
🌤
METAR & Weather
Live METAR for any ICAO. Wind direction with visual arrow, cloud coverage color-coded by severity, QNH, temperature, dew point.
🗺
VATSIM ATC
See active controllers on your route. DEL, GND, TWR, APP, CTR — tap any station to read the full ATIS text.
🗺
Live Map
Satellite or street map with your OFP route overlay. Real-time aircraft position updates as you fly.
